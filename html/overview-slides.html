Who gets in

Five rules filter teams more than anything else

1

A DTU person on the team. Skylab funding, programmes and facilities need a DTU student or employee. Clinicians need a DTU co-founder, or BETA.HEALTH.

2

Open Door is the only way in. The Startup Programme has no application form. Teams pitch at an Open-Door Coaching Session.

3

Researcher grants are staff-only. Discovery needs Stage 1 and PoC needs Stage 2, and both are for DTU employees only.

4

Time the CVR. InnoExplorer only funds projects before a CVR exists. Skylab Funding needs one within 3 months of the award.

5

Some offers need a company. Life Science Academy, SkyFactory, MedTech Growth, BII and Innofounder all need a CVR.

Sources: skylab.dtu.dk, tuh.dtu.dk, DTU course base, internal TUH programme plan · checked 1 Oct 2026 · 3

These five rules explain most "can we apply?" questions. Rule 4 is the costly one: registering early can cost a team a DKK 0.5–1.5M InnoExplorer grant.

The CVR line

Registering a company is a one-way door

Before the CVR

InnoExplorer (only here), Discovery, NNF Pioneer Innovator, BETA.HEALTH, the Skylab Funding application, Stages 1–2, all courses and events.

Register

Within 3 months of a Skylab Funding award, or normally at the end of Stage 2. A verified spin-out also counts.

After the CVR

Skylab Funding payout, Innofounder (3-year clock starts), BII Venture Lab, MedTech Growth, Life Science Academy, SkyFactory.

Sources: Skylab Funding FAQ, InnoExplorer page, Innofounder guidelines Jan 2026, BII Venture Lab guidelines Apr 2026 · 8

The most expensive mistake: a researcher team registers a CVR to collect a small Skylab Funding grant and loses access to InnoExplorer. Plan the order of grants with the Innovation Partner first.

Beyond DTU

Where external rules clash with Skylab's

CVR order

Innofounder needs a company under 3 years old. An early CVR for Skylab Funding starts that clock.

Full-time

Innofounder allows at most 10 hours a week of other work. That rules out finishing a degree or a clinical job.

Investors

BII excludes teams with prior commercial investors, so a pre-seed ticket first can block BII.

IP

Hospital staff must disclose inventions to Region H Tech Trans. BETA.HEALTH and BII need clear IP.

Sources: innovationsfonden.dk (Innofounder guidelines), bii.dk (Venture Lab guidelines), regionh.dk, betahealth.dk · 9

EIT Health Jumpstarter is closed to Danish teams (RIS countries only). Venture Cup could not be verified: its site was down on 1 Oct 2026.

Design choice

Which academic activities belong on the map?

Option A

Every DTU innovation course

Complete, but it turns the map into a catalogue that goes stale quickly.

Option B · chosen

Courses with a clinical or venture hand-off

They must work with clinicians, or produce a team or prototype that could become a startup, and have an owner who keeps dates current. Seven courses plus the MSc thesis.

Option C

No courses at all

Cleanest, but it hides the main way students arrive.
